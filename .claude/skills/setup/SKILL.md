# Setup

Offer the three parts below and let the user pick one, some or all. Each takes about 15 to 20 minutes. They can stop at any point: save what you have, note where you stopped in `current_state.md`, and resume there next time.

Ask one question at a time. Skip a question they have already answered. Follow up when an answer is vague or contradicts an earlier one. "I don't know" is a valid answer; record it as unsure. Keep their wording.

If they hand over a document that answers some questions, such as goals, a job rubric or a past review, read it first and ask only what is left.

Show the finished documents and ask what to change. Save only after they say yes.

## Part 1: Who you are

1. Not your title, your core identity. What kind of professional are you?
2. What work feels like play?
3. What work drains you? What would you never do again?
4. What do you want to be known for in ten years?
5. What do you not want to be known for?
6. How do you define success, in your own terms?
7. What does "enough" look like?
8. What would you trade to advance?
9. What would you not trade?
10. What would make you walk away?
11. What limits are you working within: time, energy, family, money, life stage?
12. Describe a typical week in your ideal role.

Writes `foundation/career_intent.md` (what they are aiming for, how they want to be known, ideal role) and `foundation/values_and_constraints.md` (values, what enough looks like, what they won't trade, limits). Date each statement so later entries can cite it.

## Part 2: This year's goals

1. What are your official goals?
2. Why do they matter to you?
3. What does success look like for each one?
4. How will other people see it?
5. What are you really aiming for that isn't in the official goals?
6. What are you not doing this year?
7. What are you giving up?
8. What limits you this year?
9. What would make the year feel successful even if you missed the goals?
10. What would make it feel like a failure even if you hit them all?

Also ask when their review year starts and ends.

Writes `goals/annual_goals.md`: the review year dates, official goals with success signs, unofficial goals, what they are not doing, tradeoffs, what success and failure feel like, and an empty Unplanned work table (date, work, why it came up, log entry). For a new year, move last year's goals to `goals/annual_goals_YYYY.md` first, using the year they ended.

## Part 3: Your job and your manager

1. What does your company say your role is judged on? If there is a written rubric, ask for it.
2. How does your manager like to work, and what matters most to them?
3. What would your manager say you are good at?
4. What would they say to work on?
5. What would they say to focus on this year?
6. Do they advocate for you? How do you know?
7. Where are you two out of step?
8. What would make it work better?
9. How well do you understand what they expect, 1 to 10?

Writes `foundation/role_expectations.md` (the written rubric word for word if there is one, and what they believe really counts) and the manager's entry in `capture/people.md`.

## Finish

After any part, create or update `profile.md` (identity, what energizes and drains them, limits) and `current_state.md`. Then say in two lines what they can do next: tell you things as they happen, or drop in a weekly summary.
