# Career review

## 1. Read everything for the period

Ask which review it is and the dates if unclear; `goals/annual_goals.md` has the review year. Read every log entry, receipt, upload and goal for the period, plus `foundation/`, `profile.md` and `synthesis/patterns.md`. Read the entries themselves, not only summaries of them.

Recount any total from the entries. If two files disagree, say so and use the entries.

## 2. Interview

Ask one question at a time. Skip what the record already answers and say what it shows instead. Offer to skip the interview.

**Quarter**
1. What moved forward?
2. What felt busy but didn't matter?
3. What got too much attention? Too little?
4. What surprised you?
5. What are you avoiding?
6. What should change or stop next quarter?
7. What drained you, and what gave you energy?
8. Who is closer than before, and who is further away?
9. If one thing moves next quarter, what should it be?

**Mid-year:** the quarter questions for the half, plus: which goals should change?

**Year**
1. What are you most proud of? What are you proud of that nobody saw?
2. Where did you fall short, and what was in your control?
3. What drained you? What lit you up?
4. What hard conversation or decision did you put off?
5. What feedback changed what you do? What feedback felt wrong?
6. What would you not repeat?
7. If this year repeated ten times, would you be satisfied?
8. What did you learn about yourself?
9. What is unfinished that still matters? What are you letting go of?

## 3. Write the draft

Show it before saving to `reviews/YYYY-MM-DD_kind.md`, dated the day you write it.

- **Quarter:** what moved, what didn't, patterns, next quarter's top three, what to stop.
- **Mid-year and year-end:** summary; each goal with its evidence and an honest status; unplanned work; wins by impact; skills shown, using `goals/receipts.md` with scope as recorded; feedback themes; what fell short; next focus.
- **Across years:** what grew, what keeps coming back, what closed.

A goal with no evidence is "no evidence on record", not "behind" or "not started". Cite the dated entry behind each claim. Give each figure the strength the record gives it: "reported by the team lead" is neither measured nor an estimate. A conflict stays open until the user settles it.

### A version to send

Only when asked, as a separate file ending `_to_send.md`. Follow "Anything other people will see" in `CLAUDE.md`, and:

- Only entries dated inside the period. Later feedback or wins don't count, even if they help.
- If an earlier review got something wrong, tell the user. Don't correct it in this version.

## 4. Update the record

Update `synthesis/patterns.md` with confirmed patterns and mark ones that stopped. Update `profile.md` where something lasting changed. Put next quarter's top three in `current_state.md` and move what is finished to `archive.md`.
