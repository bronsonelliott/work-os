# Monthly log

1. Read every log entry and upload dated in the month, the open items from last month's log, and the goals.
2. Show a draft in the `work_YYYY.md` format from what is already there, with the date of the entry behind each line.
3. Ask only about what the draft doesn't cover, one question at a time:
   - What shipped that isn't here?
   - What changed because of it, and for whom?
   - What work did nobody see?
   - What didn't work?
   - What surprised you?
   - What is still open?
4. Save the month, replacing any partial entry for it. Keep everything the partial entry said.
5. Offer any new wins for the wins log, and add unplanned work to the goals table.
6. Say whether an item has been open for two months or more, and anything from "What to notice" in `CLAUDE.md`.

If there is nothing on record for the month, run it as an interview. A short answer is fine.
