# Quick Start - 5 Minutes to Get Running

Welcome to your Work Operating System. This runs entirely through Claude Code—you'll never manually edit files. You just talk to Claude.

## Step 1: Set Your Foundation (20 minutes)

Say to Claude Code:
```
Run career identity interview
```

Claude will ask you ~12 questions about:
- Who you are professionally (your identity, not your title)
- What work energizes vs drains you
- What success means to you
- What you're not willing to trade
- Your constraints and non-negotiables

Answer conversationally. Claude will turn your responses into your foundation documents (values, constraints, principles). No manual work.

**Expected output:** 5 foundation documents populated with your responses

---

## Step 2: Set Your Goals (20 minutes)

Say to Claude Code:
```
Run goal setting interview
```

Claude will ask you ~10 questions about:
- Your official goals for this year
- Your shadow goals (what you're really optimizing for)
- What success looks like
- Your tradeoffs and constraints

Answer honestly. Claude will populate your annual goals + quarterly focus.

**Expected output:** FY26 goals (declared + shadow) + Q1 focus

---

## Step 3: Start Using It

You can use either **natural language** or **slash commands** (both work the same way).

**Every month (30 minutes):**
```
Run monthly log
```
or `/monthly-log`

Claude asks about what shipped, impact, invisible work, failures, surprises. You answer. Claude writes formatted entry.

**Anytime (2-5 minutes):**
```
Do my check-in
```
or `/check-in`

Quick reflection: energy level, wins, friction, what's next.

**Before your 1:1 (5 minutes):**
```
Prep me for my 1:1 with [manager name]
```
or `/prep-meeting` or `/prep-meeting "Sarah"`

Claude reads your recent work, generates meeting prep, shows you the output.

**When you get feedback (2-3 minutes):**
```
Log this feedback
```
or `/log-feedback`

Claude asks: what was said, who said it, your take. Logs it.

**When you ship something (3-5 minutes):**
```
Log this win
```
or `/log-win` or `/log-win "Shipped the new dashboard"`

Claude asks: what, why it mattered, who benefited, evidence. Logs it.

---

## That's the Setup

You're done. Everything else is optional or happens automatically.

---

## What Happens in the Background

Claude automatically:
- Stores everything you capture
- Recognizes patterns (same feedback 3x = pattern)
- Detects blind spots (feedback vs your self-perception)
- Flags drift (stated goals vs actual work)
- Updates your memory with insights
- Synthesizes quarterly and annually
- Keeps everything in sync

You just talk to Claude. That's it.

## Common First Questions

**"Do I need daily check-ins?"**
No. Check-ins are anytime, not daily. Monthly log is the baseline. Check in whenever you want.

**"What if I miss a month?"**
Resume forward. Don't backfill. Just say "Help me restart" and Claude guides you back in.

**"Can I see the files?"**
Yes! Say "Show me my [document name]". But you don't need to edit them manually. Claude does.

**"Can I upload past performance reviews?"**
Yes! Upload the file and say "Extract patterns from this review." Claude extracts patterns and synthesizes across reviews.

**"What's the minimum viable usage?"**
Just monthly log. Everything else is bonus. System still works.

**"When should I do the interviews?"**
Career identity + goal setting: Start (first 20-40 minutes)
Year-in-review: End of year (30-45 minutes)
Quarterly reflection: End of each quarter (15-20 minutes)
Manager relationship: Whenever needed (15 minutes)

**"What if my career changes mid-year?"**
Say "Run career identity interview" or "Run goal setting interview" again. Claude updates everything.
