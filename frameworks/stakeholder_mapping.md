# Stakeholder Mapping Framework

Acknowledges advancement requires relationships. Maps your network strategically.

## Relationship Categories

**Sponsor:** Executive advocating for you, can influence advancement
**Ally:** Trust you, support you, but limited executive power
**Neutral:** Professional, no strong signal
**Blocker:** Works against you or creates friction
**Unknown:** Haven't determined yet

## Assessment

For each important stakeholder:
- Current relationship category
- Trust level
- Value to your goals
- Next action to strengthen

## Key Insight

**Promotion rarely happens without sponsors.** Technical excellence gets to L3 (Senior IC). L4+ (Principal/Staff) requires advocate with power.

## Pattern Recognition

**Health signals:**
- 2-3+ executive sponsors
- More allies than blockers
- Few unknowns (you know where people stand)

**Risk signals:**
- No executive sponsors
- More blockers than allies
- Isolated (mostly neutral relationships)

## Application

**Quarterly:** Review relationship status
**Annual:** Assess sponsor gap, plan relationship building
