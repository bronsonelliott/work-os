# Career Capital Framework

Cal Newport's model: Career optionality comes from three forms of capital.

## Three Forms of Capital

**Skills Capital**
- What can you do?
- Technical + leadership capabilities
- Depth vs breadth tradeoff

**Relationship Capital**
- Who do you know?
- Sponsors, allies, peers, network
- Trust and credibility with stakeholders

**Reputation Capital**
- What are you known for?
- Brand in industry / company
- How do people describe you in your absence

## Assessment

For each:
- Current state (what you have)
- Target state (where you want to be)
- Gap (what's missing)

## Key Principle

Advancement requires building capital in all three areas simultaneously. Many people focus only on skills (L2 Problem), missing relationships and reputation.

## Example

**Current:**
- Skills: Advanced Python, SQL, analytics (strong)
- Relationships: Team allies but no executive sponsors
- Reputation: "Analytics expert" but not known outside team

**Gap:**
- Need Python + AI expertise (skills gap)
- Need 2 executive sponsors (relationship gap)
- Need company-wide visibility (reputation gap)

**3-Year Target:**
- Principal IC level Python + AI implementation
- 2-3 executive sponsors
- Known as "AI strategy expert" across company
