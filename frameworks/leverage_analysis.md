# Leverage Analysis Framework

2x2 Matrix: Impact vs Effort. Makes visible what work is actually valuable.

## Four Quadrants

**High Impact + Low Effort = SUPERPOWER**
- Do more of this
- Your comparative advantage
- Career accelerant

**High Impact + High Effort = SYSTEMATIZE**
- Worth the effort OR
- Delegate after building, or
- Develop capability if strategic

**Low Impact + Low Effort = AUTOMATE/DELETE**
- Automate if recurs
- Delete if unnecessary
- Don't waste mental energy

**Low Impact + High Effort = STOP**
- Career killer quadrant
- Consuming time without building capital
- Address root cause

## Usage

**Monthly:** Rate work by impact (1-5) and effort (1-5)
**Quarterly:** Analyze what % in each quadrant
**Goal:** Move toward High Impact quadrants

## Key Insight

Advancement isn't about working harder—it's about higher-leverage work. Many stuck ICs work 60 hours/week in Low Impact quadrants.
