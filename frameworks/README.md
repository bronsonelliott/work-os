# Frameworks - Analytical Tools for Career

This folder contains analytical frameworks to help you understand yourself and make better career decisions. You don't need to use all of them—use selectively based on your needs.

---

## Framework Quick Reference

| Framework | Core Question | Blind Spot It Reveals | When to Use |
|-----------|---|---|---|
| **Brag Document** | What have I actually accomplished? | You're doing more than you remember | Review prep, documenting wins |
| **Impact Ladder** | What scope level am I operating at? | You're solving problems, not defining them | Goal-setting, project selection |
| **Leverage Analysis** | What should I do more/less of? | 40% of your time is low-value work | Prioritization, time management |
| **Career Capital** | What assets am I building? | Building skills but not relationships | 3-year goal assessment |
| **Energy ROI** | What's sustainable? | High-impact work is draining | Burnout prevention, sustainability |
| **Stakeholder Mapping** | Who influences my success? | You have allies but no sponsors | Relationship building, advancement |

---

## How to Use Frameworks

**Monthly:** Brag Document + Energy ROI
- Keep winning, track energy

**Quarterly:** Add Impact Ladder + Leverage Analysis + Stakeholder Mapping
- Assess your level, priorities, relationships

**Annually:** All frameworks for full assessment
- Career capital comprehensive review

---

## Usage Tips

- Don't apply all frameworks to all work (overwhelm)
- Use selectively based on current question
- Combine frameworks for richer insight
- Reference your 3-year career target
- Update as you progress

---

## Key Principle

These frameworks transform vague feelings ("I should get promoted") into concrete diagnostic assessments ("I operate at Level 2, need Level 4").

---

## Anti-Pattern

Don't use frameworks for judgment. Use them for clarity.

See individual framework files for detailed guides and examples.
