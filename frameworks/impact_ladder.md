# Impact Ladder Framework

IC Progression Model: How to move from task executor to strategic director.

## Five Levels of Scope

**Level 1: Task Executor**
- Completes assigned work well
- Delivers quality, follows processes
- Needs clear direction

**Level 2: Problem Solver**  
- Identifies problems independently
- Proposes and implements solutions
- Owns outcomes with minimal supervision

**Level 3: Problem Definer**
- Sees problems others don't
- Frames problems for others
- Delegates execution while owning strategy

**Level 4: Multiplier**
- Makes entire team 2-10x more effective
- Builds systems others use
- Influences without authority

**Level 5: Direction Setter**
- Sets strategic direction for domain
- Shapes what problems get solved
- Builds organizational capability

## Application

**Project Selection:** Choose projects at next level to build capability

**Goal-Setting:** "Operate consistently at Level 4" is clearer than "Get promoted"

**Feedback:** "You're strong at L2-3 but need more L4 scope" is actionable
