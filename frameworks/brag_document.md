# Brag Document Framework

Based on Julia Evans' concept: https://jvns.ca/blog/brag-documents/

## What & Why

**Problem:** Brain doesn't archive work details. Reviews require months-long summary you can't produce.

**Solution:** Real-time documentation of accomplishments with evidence.

## Entry Template

```
## [Accomplishment Title]

**What:** [Specific thing you did]

**Why It Mattered:** [Impact, value, benefit]

**Who Benefited:** [Stakeholders]

**Evidence:** [Metrics, quotes, links, documentation]

**Skills Demonstrated:** [What this shows about you]
```

## Quality Bar

**Good:** "Built subscription reporting pipeline reducing reporting time from 4 hours to 15 minutes, now used by 3 teams"

**Bad:** "Worked on analytics improvements"

## Usage

- Capture wins real-time (while memory fresh)
- Add evidence as available (metrics, quotes, adoption)
- Review monthly for missed wins
- Pull into review prep 1-2 times per year

See capture/wins_ledger.md for where your brag documents live.

## Integration

Feeds into:
- Review prep (pull wins for talking points)
- Promotion packets (concrete evidence)
- Career capital assessment (evidence of skills)
