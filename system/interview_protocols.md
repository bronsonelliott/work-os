# Interview Protocols - Scripts and Synthesis Rules

This document defines how to conduct each interview type, what questions to ask, and which files get populated from responses.

## General Interview Principles

**Conversational flow:**
- Ask questions one at a time
- Capture responses in user's own words
- Don't require formal structure from user
- You handle formatting afterward

**Synthesis:**
- After interview, generate structured documents per synthesis targets
- Show user what you created
- Ask for edits before saving
- Apply changes
- Execute cascading updates

**Tone:**
- Curious, not judgmental
- Direct, not corporate
- Supportive, not coddling
- Pattern-seeking, not prescriptive

## CAREER IDENTITY INTERVIEW

**File:** `interviews/career_identity.md`

**Length:** 12 questions, 20-30 minutes

**When to Use:** First time setting up, or when user says they've changed direction

**Questions (ask one at a time):**

1. "What kind of professional are you? Not your title—your core identity. Are you a builder? A strategist? A teacher? A problem-solver? What comes first?"

2. "What work feels like play to you? When do you lose track of time?"

3. "What work feels like punishment? What drains you? What would you never want to do again?"

4. "What do you want to be known for in 10 years? Your professional legacy?"

5. "What do you NOT want to be known for? What would make you feel like you wasted your career?"

6. "How do you define success at work? Not your company's definition—yours."

7. "What does 'enough' look like for you? When would you know you've succeeded?"

8. "What are you willing to trade for career advancement? Time? Energy? Work-life balance? Family time?"

9. "What are you NOT willing to trade? What are your non-negotiables?"

10. "What would make you walk away from this role or company? What's your red line?"

11. "What constraints are you working within? Energy? Time? Family? Life stage? Financial?"

12. "If you could design your ideal role, what would it look like? Walk me through a typical week."

**Synthesis Targets:**

After capturing all responses, populate these files:

1. **foundation/values_and_constraints.md**
   - Core Values: from Q1, Q6
   - How I Define Success: from Q6
   - What "Enough" Looks Like: from Q7
   - Lines I Will Not Cross: from Q9, Q10
   - What I'm Not Willing to Trade: from Q9
   - My Non-Negotiables: from Q10
   - Constraints I'm Working Within: from Q11

2. **foundation/career_intent_FY26.md** (the Why section)
   - What I'm Optimizing For: from Q1, Q2, Q4
   - How I Want to Be Remembered: from Q4, Q5

3. **foundation/principles.md**
   - My Professional Identity: from Q1
   - How I Define Success: from Q6
   - My Relationship With Work: from Q2, Q3

4. **memory.md**
   - Add: "Career Identity Insights: [key themes from interview]"

**Post-Interview:**
- Show user: "Here's what I captured about who you are professionally. Want to edit anything?"
- Apply edits
- Save all files
- Confirm: "Your career identity foundation is set. Ready to set goals next?"

## GOAL-SETTING INTERVIEW

**File:** `interviews/goal_setting.md`

**Length:** 10 questions, 20-30 minutes

**When to Use:** Start of fiscal year, or when revising goals mid-year

**Questions:**

1. "What are your official goals for this year? What did your company/manager ask you to achieve?"

2. "Why do these goals matter to you? What's your real motivation?"

3. "What does success actually look like for each goal? Get specific."

4. "How will others SEE that you succeeded? What observable signals or evidence?"

5. "What are you really optimizing for that isn't in your official goals?" [This is the shadow goals question]

6. "What are you explicitly NOT doing this year? What are you saying no to?"

7. "What tradeoffs are you making this year? What will you sacrifice or deprioritize?"

8. "What constraints are you working within this year? Time, resources, energy?"

9. "What would make this year feel successful, even if official goals aren't fully met?"

10. "What would make this year feel like failure, even if all official goals ARE met?"

**Synthesis Targets:**

1. **goals/annual_goals_FY26.md**
   - Official Goals: from Q1, Q3, Q4
   - Shadow Goals (what user really cares about): from Q5
   - What Success Looks Like: from Q3, Q9
   - What Failure Looks Like: from Q10

2. **foundation/career_intent_FY26.md**
   - What I'm Optimizing For This Year: from Q5 (shadow goals)
   - Explicit Tradeoffs: from Q7
   - Constraints: from Q8

3. **goals/quarterly_focus.md** (Q1)
   - Derive top 3 priorities from annual goals
   - Note which are official vs shadow

4. **memory.md**
   - Add: "FY26 Priorities: [key themes]"
   - Add: "Shadow Goals: [what user really wants]"

**Post-Interview:**
- Show: "Here are your FY26 goals—declared and shadow."
- Ask: "Want to edit?"
- Apply edits
- Save
- Confirm: "Goals set. Quarterly focus updated. Ready to start using the system."

## YEAR-IN-REVIEW INTERVIEW

**File:** `interviews/year_in_review.md`

**Length:** 15 questions, 30-45 minutes

**When to Use:** End of calendar year or fiscal year

**Questions:**

1. "Tell me about the past year—highlights first. What are you most proud of?"

2. "What are you proud of that no one else sees? What invisible work matters to you?"

3. "What surprised you about your own performance this year?"

4. "Where did you underdeliver and why? Be honest—what was within your control vs not?"

5. "What drained you the most this year? What energy drain was persistent?"

6. "What energized you? What lit you up?"

7. "Where did you avoid hard conversations or decisions? What did you postpone?"

8. "What feedback landed this year? What feedback changed your behavior?"

9. "What feedback didn't land? What felt misaligned or wrong?"

10. "What would you absolutely not repeat under any circumstances?"

11. "If this year repeated ten times, would you be satisfied with that life?"

12. "What did you learn about yourself? Strengths, weaknesses, limits, patterns?"

13. "What would you tell yourself 12 months ago if you could? What advice?"

14. "What's unfinished that matters? What's still open?"

15. "What are you letting go of as you move into next year?"

**Synthesis Targets:**

1. **reviews/year_end_review.md**
   - Overall Summary: from Q1-3, create narrative
   - Key Wins: from Q1, Q2
   - Misses/Gaps: from Q4
   - Growth & Learning: from Q12
   - What Drained/Energized Me: from Q5, Q6
   - Feedback Themes: from Q8, Q9
   - Next Year Focus: from Q14, Q15

2. **synthesis/year_at_a_glance.md**
   - Full narrative arc of the year
   - Quarterly progression
   - Major wins
   - Key learnings
   - How year changed you

3. **synthesis/pattern_recognition.md**
   - Update all sections: Recurring Themes, Persistent Gaps, Energy Patterns, etc.

4. **memory.md**
   - Add: "Year Insights: [key themes]"
   - Add: "Learning From Past Year: [key insights]"

**Post-Interview Follow-Up:**
- Ask: "Want me to go deeper on any area? Highlights, challenges, feedback, learnings?"
- If yes → Ask follow-up questions on that area
- Synthesize follow-ups into documents

**Post-Interview:**
- Show: "Here's your year-end review and year-at-a-glance narrative."
- Ask: "Want to edit?"
- Apply edits
- Save
- Confirm: "Year-end review complete. [key insights noted]"

## QUARTERLY REFLECTION INTERVIEW

**File:** `interviews/quarterly_reflection.md`

**Length:** 10 questions, 15-20 minutes

**When to Use:** End of each quarter

**Questions:**

1. "What actually moved forward this quarter? What shipped?"

2. "What felt busy but didn't actually matter? What was low-value?"

3. "Where did you over-rotate? What got too much attention?"

4. "Where did you under-invest? What was neglected that shouldn't be?"

5. "What surprised you this quarter?"

6. "What are you avoiding? Decisions, conversations, problems?"

7. "What needs to change next quarter? What should be different?"

8. "What should you stop doing entirely?"

9. "What energy patterns emerged? What consistently drained vs energized you?"

10. "What stakeholder signals did you notice? Who's closer, who's further away?"

**Synthesis Targets:**

1. **goals/quarterly_focus.md** (next quarter)
   - Top 3 Priorities: from Q1, Q4, Q7
   - What Needs to Change: from Q7
   - What to Stop Doing: from Q8
   - What Would Make Quarter a Win: derived from patterns

2. **capture/energy_log.md** (if not already tracked)
   - Add: patterns from Q9

3. **capture/stakeholder_log.md** (if not already tracked)
   - Update based on Q10 observations

4. **synthesis/pattern_recognition.md**
   - Update: Recurring Themes, Energy Patterns, Stakeholder Patterns

5. **memory.md**
   - Add: "Q[X] Insights: [key themes]"

**Post-Interview:**
- Show: "Here's your Q[X+1] focus based on the quarter."
- Ask: "Want to edit?"
- Apply edits
- Save
- Confirm: "Q[X+1] focus set."

## MANAGER RELATIONSHIP INTERVIEW

**File:** `interviews/manager_relationship.md`

**Length:** 10 questions, 15 minutes

**When to Use:** Mid-year or when manager relationship changes

**Questions:**

1. "What's your manager's management style? How do they like to work?"

2. "What does your manager think you're good at? What's their perception?"

3. "What does your manager think you need to improve on? Their honest take?"

4. "What's your manager's background and priorities? What matters to them?"

5. "How frequently do you talk? How accessible are they?"

6. "What would your manager say you should focus on this year?"

7. "Does your manager advocate for you? Do you feel supported for advancement?"

8. "What's the biggest tension or misalignment in your relationship?"

9. "What would make this relationship work better? What would help?"

10. "On a scale of 1-10, how well do you understand your manager's expectations of you?"

**Synthesis Targets:**

1. **foundation/role_expectations.md**
   - Official Expectations (what company says): from Q6
   - Manager's Expectations (what manager says): from Q2, Q3, Q6
   - Actual Expectations (reality): from user's interpretation
   - My Perception vs Manager's Perception: from Q3 vs user experience

2. **capture/stakeholder_log.md**
   - Add/Update manager entry:
     - Trust signals: from Q7, Q9
     - Risk signals: from Q8
     - Accessibility: from Q5
     - Recent interaction: current date

3. **memory.md**
   - Add: "Manager Dynamic: [key themes]"
   - Add: "Manager Relationship Status: [assessment]"

**Post-Interview:**
- Show: "Here's your updated role expectations and manager relationship profile."
- Ask: "Want to edit?"
- Apply edits
- Save
- Confirm: "Manager relationship documented."

## Interview Synthesis Notes

### Quality of Synthesis

**Do:**
- Capture user's own words/phrasing where possible
- Preserve nuance and complexity
- Note when user is uncertain
- Include both positives and development areas
- Use quotes when especially revealing

**Don't:**
- Paraphrase everything (preserve language)
- Over-interpret (stick to what was said)
- Force answers into categories (let ambiguity exist)
- Judge or evaluate (just document)
- Change user's meaning

### Handling Unclear or Vague Responses

- If response is very brief: Ask "Say more about that? What do you mean?"
- If response seems inconsistent: Gently note: "That sounds different from what you said about [related topic]. Help me understand?"
- If user says "I don't know": That's valid. Capture: "[User unsure]"
- If response is tangential: Gently redirect: "How does that relate to [question]?"

### After Interview Synthesis

Always:
1. Show user what you generated
2. Ask: "Want to edit anything?"
3. Apply edits before saving
4. Save to files
5. Execute cascading updates
6. Update memory.md
7. Confirm completion

Never:
- Assume user wants you to just save (show first)
- Force user to accept synthesis as-is
- Save without showing
- Skip memory.md update
- Leave cascade updates unexecuted

## Interview Troubleshooting

**User says "I don't know" to a question:**
→ That's valid. Capture: "[User uncertain on this]"
→ Don't force an answer
→ Move to next question

**User's response is very long:**
→ That's good, let them talk
→ You'll format it after
→ Ask follow-up if clarification needed

**User gets emotional:**
→ That's normal and valid
→ Acknowledge: "That sounds important"
→ Continue interview at their pace

**User says interview feels pointless:**
→ Ask: "What would make this more useful?"
→ Adjust questions if appropriate
→ Or: "Trust the process. You'll see the value in generated documents."

**User wants to edit a lot:**
→ That's fine, apply edits
→ Can iterate until they're satisfied
→ Then save

**User says they've changed their mind about something:**
→ Capture new answer
→ Ask: "Should I update what we captured before, or keep both?"
→ Honor their evolution

## Interview Document Structure

Each interview file (in `/interviews/`) contains:

1. **Interview Name:** Clear title
2. **Questions:** Complete list of questions to ask
3. **Purpose:** Why this interview exists
4. **Frequency:** How often to run
5. **Synthesis Targets:** Which files get populated and how
6. **Typical Duration:** Time estimate
7. **Follow-up Opportunities:** Optional deeper dives
8. **Post-Interview Process:** Steps after capturing responses

1. Load interview file

6. Show user what you created
