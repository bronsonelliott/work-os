# Work Operating System

A personal career management system that runs entirely through conversational AI. You talk to Claude Code—Claude handles the documentation, synthesis, and insights.

## What This Is

This is your professional operating system. Instead of scattered notes, manual journaling, or forgetting what you shipped last quarter, you have a conversational interface that:

- **Captures your work** through natural conversation (monthly logs, check-ins, wins, feedback)
- **Detects patterns** automatically (recurring feedback, blind spots, goal drift)
- **Synthesizes insights** without manual effort (quarterly reflections, year-in-review)
- **Keeps you aligned** between your stated goals and actual work

You never manually edit files. You just talk to Claude.

## Core Philosophy

**You're having a conversation, not managing a system.**

- No templates to fill out
- No manual file editing (unless you want to)
- No daily obligations
- Forward momentum only (no backfilling)
- Minimum viable usage: just monthly logs

## What You Get

### Foundation Documents
- Professional identity (who you are, not your title)
- Core values and principles
- Constraints and non-negotiables
- Success criteria

### Work Tracking
- Monthly logs (what shipped, impact, invisible work, failures)
- Quick check-ins (energy, wins, friction)
- Win logs (evidence of impact)
- Feedback logs (what others are telling you)

### Intelligence Layer
Claude automatically:
- Recognizes patterns (same feedback 3x = pattern)
- Detects blind spots (feedback vs self-perception)
- Flags drift (stated goals vs actual work)
- Synthesizes quarterly and annually
- Prepares you for 1:1s and reviews

## Philosophy

This system assumes:
- Your stated goals ≠ your actual goals (shadow goals exist)
- You can't spot your own patterns (need external synthesis)
- Consistency beats intensity (monthly > daily that you skip)
- Evidence > memory (you'll forget your wins)
- Career management should be conversational, not administrative
