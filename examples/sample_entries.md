# Sample entries

Everything here is made up. It shows the level of detail that helps later.

## A win with enough evidence

```
## 2026-03-13: Vendor spend dashboard
**What:** Built and shipped a vendor spend dashboard.
**Why it mattered:** Replaced a manual report the accounts payable team built every week.
**Who benefited:** Accounts payable (4 people), the controller.
**Evidence:** 6 hours a week saved, reported by the team lead. Opened by 11 people in week one.
**Goal:** Goal 2, cut manual reporting
```
