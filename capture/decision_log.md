# Decision Log

*Yes/no decisions with tradeoffs documented*
*Time estimate: 2-3 minutes per entry*
*Added through: "Log this decision" command*

---

## Entry Template

```
## [Date]

**Decision:** [What did you decide? Yes to what? No to what?]

**Reasoning:** [Why this choice over alternatives?]

**Tradeoff:** [What are you giving up by choosing this?]

**Reflections:** [Any second thoughts or changes as you live with it?]

**Alignment:** [Does this violate any stated constraints?]
```

---

## Sample Entries

```
## 2026-01-02

**Decision:** Said YES to VP's request to lead Analytics strategy for her org

**Reasoning:** High visibility opportunity, aligns with career goal, VP is potential sponsor, strategic value

**Tradeoff:** 
- 2-3 hours/week more work
- Less time for individual contributor technical depth
- Risk of overcommitting

**Reflections:** Glad I said yes, excited about it. Pushes me toward management track which I want to explore.

**Alignment:** Aligned with career intent (optimize for advancement + leadership). Check constraint: "won't work weekends" - so far fine.

---

## 2025-12-20

**Decision:** Said NO to speaking panel at conference "Women in Data"

**Reasoning:** 
- Would require presentation prep (20+ hours)
- Month is already full with pipeline project
- Can do this next year when less busy
- Not critical for career goals

**Tradeoff:** 
- Pass on visibility opportunity
- Conference organizer disappointed
- Might not get asked again?

**Reflections:** Right call. I'm already at capacity. Can do thought leadership in other ways.

**Alignment:** Aligned with constraint "only take on work aligned with 3-year goal". This year it's not a priority.
```
