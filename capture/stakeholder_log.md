# Stakeholder Log

*Relationship tracking with trust and risk signals*
*Time estimate: 3-5 minutes*
*Added through: Quarterly reflection, ongoing observation*

---

## Entry Template

```
## [Person Name]

**Relationship:** [Sponsor | Ally | Neutral | Blocker | Unknown]

**Trust Level:** [How much do they trust you?]

**Value to You:** [Why this relationship matters]

**Last Interaction:** [Date and brief summary]

**Next Action:** [What you could do to strengthen relationship]

**Signals:** [Trust signals or risk signals]
```

---

## Relationship Categories

**Sponsor:** Executive advocating for you, wants you to succeed, can influence advancement
**Ally:** Strong collaborator, trusts you, supports your goals but no executive power
**Neutral:** Professional, polite, no strong signal either way
**Blocker:** Actively working against you or creating friction
**Unknown:** Haven't determined yet where they stand
