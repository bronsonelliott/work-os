# Feedback Log

*Verbatim feedback capture with context and patterns*
*Time estimate: 2-3 minutes per entry*
*Added through: "Log this feedback" command*

---

## Entry Template

```
## [Date]

**Feedback:** "[Exact quote if possible, otherwise close paraphrase]"

**Source:** [Who gave it] | [When/Where/Context] | [Date]

**My Interpretation:** [What you think they meant]

**Action Taken/Planned:** [What you did or will do about it, or "None yet"]
```

---

## Sample Entries

```
## 2026-01-02

**Feedback:** "Your communication skills improved a lot this year. I notice you're better at explaining technical concepts to non-technical people."

**Source:** Manager Sarah | During 1:1 | 2026-01-02

**My Interpretation:** This is positive feedback about communication improvement. She specifically noticed technical-to-business translation skill.

**Action Taken/Planned:** None yet - just positive reinforcement. Will keep doing this.

## 2026-01-01

**Source:** Peer feedback from Jake | During sprint retro | 2025-12-20

**Action Taken/Planned:** Talked with team about what they could own. Delegated dashboard maintenance to junior analyst.
```

## Integration Points

Sources: Feedback as it happens (log immediately while fresh)
Analyzed for: Recurring themes (3+ same feedback = pattern), blind spots, contradictions with self-perception
Updates: pattern_recognition.md (when theme repeats), memory.md (if important pattern)

Claude will:
- Ask for exact wording (verbatim better than paraphrased)
- Check for recurring themes
- Flag if contradicts stated strengths
- Track action taken

## Tips

- Log immediately while memory fresh
- Use exact quotes when possible
- Include context (who, when, why they said it)
- Your interpretation matters (what you think they meant)
- Action tracking: do you address this or consciously defer?
